1. Win Rate Degradation & Pursuit Efficiency

30.1% Win Rate → 42.5%+ Target

Degraded Metric: Acquisition win rate sits at 30.1% (112 won / 260 lost).
Correlated Data Factors:
• Lead Grade: Grades A/B convert at 47.1% / 40.5%, while C/D convert at 6.0% / 0.0%. Sales spent effort on 94 C/D pursuits producing 89 losses.
• Loss Drivers: "Specialty Experience" accounts for 45 lost deals (largest driver), whereas "Rate/Pricing" accounts for only 26.
• Channel ROI: Outbound converts at 40.9%, while LinkedIn/Paid Social converts at 0.0% (0 won / 31 pursuits).
Actionable Fix: Enforce WF-09 to auto-disqualify Grade D leads, run WF-13 consultant match before bidding on deals >$50k, and shift LinkedIn spend into Outbound.

2. Delivery Health & Risk Under-Reporting

48 Engagements <60 Health

Degraded Metric: 48 active engagements score <60 health, but only 8 are flagged "At Risk" in CRM status (a 40-engagement gap).
Correlated Data Factors:
• Consultant Seniority: Associate & Consultant engagements average 63–64 health, while Principal & Partner engagements average 77 health.
• Match Quality: Engagements with match scores <50 represent 3 unservable pursuits and elevated delivery risk.
Actionable Fix: Run WF-11 to write back zia_health_score onto deal status automatically and pair Associate delivery with Principal oversight.

3. Aging Receivables & Cash Collections

$4.19M 90+ Days Past Due

Degraded Metric: $4.19M out of $4.49M outstanding receivables (93%) are 90+ days past due across 40 invoices.
Correlated Data Factors:
• Invoice Terms: Net 15 / Net 30 collect in 36.2 days, whereas Net 60 accounts for 82% of 90+ day delinquencies.
• Projected Loss: Historical write-off rate is 5.15% ($216k projected write-off).
Actionable Fix: Run WF-07 Collections to auto-open tickets at 15 days past due, restrict Net 60 terms for non-Summit clients, and enforce retainer pre-payment for Grade C/D buyers.

4. Price Concessions vs. Talent Retention

$1.38M Discounting vs $609k Bonus Pool

Degraded Metric: 38.5% of line items carry client discounts, giving away $1.38M of list margin.
Correlated Data Factors:
• Commercial Misalignment: Discretionary discounting does not fix specialty experience gaps.
• Talent Incentive: Re-allocating 15% of given-away discount margin funds a 10% Consultant Performance Bonus Pool ($609k) for high-health engagements (≥75).
Actionable Fix: Require manager approval for discounts >5% and tie consultant bonuses to high-health delivery.
