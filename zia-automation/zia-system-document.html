Section 01

The problem in one paragraph

ZIA is a two-sided business. It sells leadership and organizational development programmes to companies, and delivers them through consultants. Revenue only happens when both sides line up — a sale without an available consultant cannot be delivered, and a consultant without demand is paid to sit idle.

A CRM for that business has to track the join between the two sides, not just a sales funnel. HubSpot's free tier is entirely capable of storing that model: custom objects properties, associations, tickets, quotes, products and forms all work. What it cannot do is act on it. Every automation endpoint returns 403.

The constraint

Free HubSpot is an excellent filing cabinet with no moving parts. It will hold anything you put in it and it will never do anything with it. No ticket is ever raised, no lead is ever routed, no overdue invoice is ever chased, and no consultant with a lapsed certificate is ever taken off the bench.

This was verified rather than assumed. The automation scope is granted on the private app and it gates nothing — the block is the plan, not the permission. Free has no automation at all; Starter offers "simple automation" capped at 50 workflows; the real workflow builder begins at Professional.

Why it reconciles instead of reacting

The score is written back with its reasoning attached. The records are synthetic. No real client or individual appears in this portal. The schema, the automation, the API behaviour and every finding below are real; the names and amounts are generated. This should be stated before anyone asks.

Where it changed the answer, not just the effort

Time saved is the least interesting benefit. The system made four questions answerable that the business previously could not ask at all.

1 · The CRM understates its own risk by six times

133 engagements score below 60. Only 22 carry the At Risk status. Health is recomputed from live delivery signals on every run; the status field is not written back from it. The flag the business filters and reports on is therefore stale by design-omission. Closing that loop is the single highest-value change available.

2 · The premium tier delivers nothing extra

Summit averages $26,402 per deal against Core's $15,361 — a 72% premium — while delivered health is flat across all three tiers (67.9 / 68.2 / 67.9, a spread of 0.36 points). Either the premium buys something this CRM does not measure, or it buys nothing. Nobody had put the two numbers side by side.

3 · Receivables have stopped moving, not slowed down

86 of 115 unpaid invoices are more than 90 days past due, worth $1,601,652, against an average time-to-pay of 37.2 days. A long tail against a short average is not slow payment — it is an absent collections process.

4 · Nearly four deals in ten are discounted

578 of 1,500 line items carry a discount — 38.5% of everything sold — averaging 10.9% off, or $1,522,504 of list value given away. No one decides to discount at that rate; it accumulates one deal at a time.

The honest limitation

This runs on a schedule, so there is lag. A paid HubSpot workflow fires in seconds; this reconciles on its next pass. That is a real trade — instant response was exchanged for zero licence cost. The reconciling design is what makes the trade safe rather than merely cheap.

Section 05

What HubSpot's paid tiers would replace

Every rule here maps to a real HubSpot feature. The reason all eleven were built by hand is that those features live across four different Hubs, and almost all begin at Professional.

Hub by hub, with what it would change here

Sales Hub Professional Professional

Replaces WF-01 and WF-02. Gives native workflows, "rotate record to owner", sequences, and deal-stage automation.

In practice

A CHRO submits the consultation form at 9:04pm. Today WF-08 raises the speed-to-lead ticket on its next pass — possibly the following morning. With Sales Hub Pro the rotation action assigns her to the East pod owner on submission, and a three-step follow-up sequence starts immediately. Research on inbound response times consistently shows contact within the first hour dominates outcomes; overnight lag is the difference between a booked call and a cold callback.

Service Hub Professional Professional

Replaces WF-05 and WF-10. Native help-desk SLAs with automatic escalation, plus built-in NPS and CSAT surveys.

In practice

The portal currently carries 174 SLA-breached tickets, and the breach is a flag this engine calculates and stamps on. With Service Hub Pro the SLA is a real timer: the clock starts when the ticket opens, the ticket changes colour as it approaches the limit, and it escalates to a manager by itself.

The 10 NPS detractor tickets are currently derived from a score already sitting in a property. Service Hub would send the survey — automatically, 30 days after an engagement ends — so the sentiment data becomes something the business collects rather than something it assumes.

Marketing Hub Professional Professional

Replaces WF-09's scoring half and the first-touch attribution model. Adds nurture email, smart forms with progressive profiling, and campaign reporting.

In practice

Attribution here is first-touch only, rolled from contact onto deal. It shows referral converting at 23.5% against paid search at 0%. But first-touch cannot see that a referral lead also attended a conference and opened four emails before buying. Marketing Hub's multi-touch attribution splits credit across the whole journey — which is what you need before moving budget, because first-touch systematically overpays whichever channel happens to arrive first.

Operations Hub Professional Professional — the pivotal one

Replaces WF-11 and the engine's whole execution model. Custom coded workflow actions, programmable automation, data quality automation, and two-way Data Sync with outside systems.

In practice

The five-signal health model cannot be built with drag-and-drop conditions — it needs real code. Operations Hub Pro lets that exact JavaScript run inside a HubSpot workflow, triggered when a ticket is raised or an invoice goes overdue, instead of on a 30-minute timer.

Data Sync also removes the invoice mirror entirely. Today seven deal properties carry invoice state copied from accounting. With Ops Hub, QuickBooks or Xero syncs two-way and the mirror stops being something anyone maintains.

Commerce Hub Add-on

Replaces WF-07. Native invoices, payment links, automated reminders and subscription billing.

In practice

There is $1,758,415 outstanding, and 86 invoices more than 90 days past due. WF-07 raises a ticket for each — but a ticket asks a human to chase. Commerce Hub emails the client a reminder with a payment link at 7, 14 and 30 days automatically. Most of that tail is not disputed debt; it is debt nobody got round to asking for. Requires a US entity and a connected processor.

Enterprise tiers Enterprise

Custom objects, predictive lead scoring, territory management, sandboxes.

In practice

Consultant certifications are currently four properties on a contact, so a person can hold only one certification with one expiry date. As a custom object each certification becomes its own record — many per consultant, each with an issuer, a scope and its own renewal date. That is the difference between "this consultant is compliant" and "this consultant is compliant for this kind of engagement, until March."

The five delivery pods WF-09 assigns are also a hand-built stand-in for Sales Enterprise territory management, which handles the same routing with real ownership rules and quota rollup.

Cost of the equivalent

Replacing all eleven rules with native features means Sales Pro + Service Pro + Marketing Pro + Operations Pro — realistically the Customer Platform Professional bundle — and Enterprise is still required for custom objects and territories. Indicatively that is in the region of $1,500–2,000 per month; treat that as a planning figure and confirm against current HubSpot pricing, which changes.

The engine's real argument is not that it is free. It is that the logic was specified, versioned and proven before any licence was bought — so the upgrade decision is now a latency and support question, not a discovery exercise.

Self-verification
